# Guide: from meeting to task list

*For people who have never used Claude or Codex. Nothing to install by hand, no files to export.*

## 1. Set up (once)

1. Install **one** of these apps and sign in:
   - **Claude**: https://claude.ai/download (open **Claude Code** inside the app)
   - **Codex**: https://chatgpt.com/codex
2. In the app, paste this sentence and press Enter:

   ```
   Install this skill for me: https://github.com/Pillar-Technology/meeting-task-clips
   ```

3. Wait until it says it is installed. If it asks permission to run something, allow it.

That is all. You never need to do this again.

## 2. After a meeting

In Google Meet, the recording lands in Google Drive, in a folder called
**Meet Recordings**. You have two ways to hand it over. Pick whichever is easier.

**Option A: paste the link**

1. In Google Drive, right-click the recording → **Share** → set **General access** to
   **Anyone with the link** → **Copy link**.
2. Paste it into the chat with one line about who is who:

   ```
   Here is today's meeting: <paste link>
   Pak Budi is my boss, I am Rina. What do I need to do?
   ```

**Option B: send the video file**

Download the recording, drag it into the chat, and write the same line about who is who.

**Optional, for better accuracy:** if someone turned on **Transcripts** during the
meeting, there is also a Google Doc ending in "- Transcript" in the same folder. Paste
its link too. It contains everyone's names, so the "who said what" is more reliable.

## 3. What you get back

In the chat, within a few minutes:

- what your boss wants overall
- **your tasks**, with deadline and details
- what was given to other people
- anything you should confirm with your boss

And in a folder on your computer:

- **MEETING_SUMMARY.html**: double-click it. It opens in your browser, with a video
  player under each task so you can replay the instruction.
- **clips/**: the same clips as separate files, to forward to a colleague.

The first video you send takes longer, because the app sets up its listening tool once.
A one-hour meeting then takes roughly 5 to 10 minutes.

## Reading the summary page

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| What the bosses need | the big picture and the decisions made |
| Tasks | the table, then one card per task: who does it, who asked, deadline, details, the boss's words, and the clip |
| Questions and answers | every question and the answer given. "Not answered" means follow up |
| Topics, in order | the meeting from start to end, with each person's contribution |
| Unclear points to confirm | copy this list into your follow-up message to your boss |

A yellow **Please confirm** note on a task means the meeting did not make clear who owns
it or what exactly is wanted. Ask before you start.

## Things you can say afterwards

- "Show only my tasks."
- "Make the clips audio-only so I can send them on WhatsApp."
- "Clip 2 starts in the middle of a sentence. Make it longer."
- "Write the summary in Bahasa Indonesia." / "…in English."
- "The person you called Unidentified speaker is Dion."
- "Combine this week's three meetings into one task list."

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "I can't open that link" | The file is private. Set sharing to **Anyone with the link**, or send the video file instead. |
| "That link is a folder" | Right-click the video itself → Share → Copy link. |
| A wrong name on a task | Tell it who is who ("Pak Budi is the boss, I am Rina") and ask it to redo the summary. |
| It answers without using the skill | Say: "Use the meeting-task-clips skill." |

## Privacy

Your video stays on your computer. Listening and clip cutting happen there. Only the
text of what was said is read by the AI. Check company rules before using it on
confidential meetings, and remember a link set to "Anyone with the link" can be opened
by anyone who has it. Switch it back to restricted when you are done.
